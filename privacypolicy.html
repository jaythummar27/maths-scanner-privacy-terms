Introduction

Welcome to Maths Scanner & AI Solution (the "App"), developed and operated by Jay Studio ("we," "us," "our"). Information We Collect

Section 02

We collect information in three ways: data provided directly by you, data processed from your camera/drawing inputs, and technical data collected automatically by integrated third-party SDKs (detailed in Section 8).

2.1 Data You Provide Manually

  • Mathematical equations, formulas, or expressions entered via our custom math keyboard
  • Handwritten math drawings created on the Draw & Solve canvas
  • Saved math solutions and problem bookmarks stored in your local History tab
  • App configuration settings (e.g., preferred language choices)

Your saved math history and bookmarks are stored exclusively on your device using Android Room (SQLite database). We do not store your private math history on our servers.

2.2 Automatically & Feature-based Data

The following are processed when you interact with specific features:

  • Camera Images (if permission granted): captured frames are processed in-memory for optical character recognition (OCR) and sent securely to Google AI API to solve math problems — see Section 4
  • Advertising Identifiers: collected by Google AdMob for ad delivery and frequency capping — see Section 6
  • App Usage & Diagnostics: screen interaction events and crash reports collected anonymously via Firebase — see Section 7
  • Device Specifications: screen density, OS build, language, and hardware model for layout scaling and compatibility

2.3 Data NOT Collected by Us

Jay Studio does not collect, store, or demand: your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, financial credentials, bank details, contact lists, or personal photos from your gallery.

AI Math Solver & Accuracy Disclaimer

Section 03

Our App utilizes Google Gemini AI and cloud artificial intelligence models to process complex mathematical expressions, recognize handwritten symbols, and generate detailed step-by-step solutions.

⚠️ Important Notice Regarding AI Generated Solutions:
Math solutions and explanations in this App are generated using artificial intelligence (Google Gemini API). While AI models are highly advanced, AI can make mistakes, misinterpret math symbols, or produce calculation errors.

How AI Requests Work

  • When you scan a photo, draw an equation, or submit a math question, the text/image content is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to Google AI services (Gemini API).
  • The AI processes the math problem and returns a formatted step-by-step answer back to your device.
  • No personally identifying user metadata (such as your account identity or personal profile) is attached to the math query payload.

AI Limitation & User Responsibility

AI technology operates on probabilistic pattern matching and reasoning algorithms. Consequently:

AI may misread handwritten math characters Step-by-step calculations may contain mistakes Solutions are intended for learning & reference only Users must double-check critical answers

Jay Studio does not guarantee 100% mathematical accuracy for every solution generated by the AI engine. Users are strongly advised to verify AI-generated answers against official textbooks or teachers before relying on them for examinations or official submissions.

Camera Permission & Usage

Section 04

The App requests the CAMERA permission strictly to enable photo-based math scanning. This allows you to point your camera at printed or written math problems in books or notes to automatically capture and solve them.

Privacy Assurance for Camera Usage:
• Camera frames are used strictly for identifying mathematical equations.
• Captured images are processed transiently in memory to perform OCR recognition.
• Images are never stored on local disk after solving, nor are they compiled into a private photo gallery.
• We do never sell, publish, or store your captured photos on public or private servers.

Camera access is completely optional. You can choose to enter math problems manually using the built-in Math Keyboard or Draw canvas without enabling camera access.

Local Data Storage & History

Section 05

To provide offline features and a convenient learning experience, the App stores certain data locally on your device using Android Room (SQLite):

  • Saved Solution History: Past solved math problems, including equations, timestamps, and step-by-step answers.
  • Custom Calculations: Saved inputs from 2D/3D geometry calculators and unit converter tools.
  • Game High Scores: Workout scores, game levels, and daily math practice streaks.

All local database files remain strictly within your device's sandbox environment. Uninstalling the App or tapping "Clear History" will permanently delete all local history.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Section 06
AdMob Disclosure: This App is supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device-level identifiers to display personalized or non-personalized ads.

Ad Types Implemented

⚡ Banner & Interstitial Ads Displayed at non-intrusive points, such as transition screens after obtaining a solution. Frequency capping is enforced to maintain a clean user experience.
🎬 Rewarded Video Ads Voluntary opt-in video ads that users can choose to watch to unlock extra AI solution hints or premium calculator features.

Data Processed by Ad Networks

You may manage or reset your advertising ID at any time via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization / Reset Advertising ID.
